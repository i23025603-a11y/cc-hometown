THE ISTANA 

Istana Besar is a historic royal palace located in Johor Bahru, built in 1866 by Sultan Abu Bakar, who is often regarded as the founder of modern Johor. The palace showcases a unique blend of Malay and Western architectural styles, reflecting the influence of colonial-era design combined with traditional elements. Overlooking the Straits of Johor, it served as the official residence of the Johor royal family and remains an important symbol of the state’s royal heritage. Today, part of the palace grounds houses the Royal Abu Bakar Museum, where visitors can learn about Johor’s history, royal artifacts, and cultural legacy. As one of Johor’s most iconic landmarks, Istana Besar represents the state’s rich history, monarchy, and cultural identity while also attracting tourists from around the world.
